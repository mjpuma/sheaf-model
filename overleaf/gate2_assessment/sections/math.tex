\section{Mathematics}
\label{sec:math}

The market loop is Gate~0 and is not restated (white paper \S3). This
layer does not rewrite \texttt{\_simulate\_window}. It is two-pass: simulate
open (\(\tau\equiv 0\)), then impose cuts, then simulate closed.

Absolute stock floors fire every hungry season even in climatology --- the
harvest calendar already drives the trough below Gate~0 competitive safety
(\(\mathrm{stu\_target}\times C_{\mathrm{ann}}\)). The crisis object on
this host is the climatology-relative ratio. For exporter \(i\), shock year
\(T\),
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:tau}
\tau_{i,t}
=
\begin{cases}
\tau^{\mathrm{on}} & t\in T \text{ and } S_{i,t}/S^{\mathrm{calm}}_{i,t} < r, \\
0 & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
\(\tau^{\mathrm{on}}=0.6\) and \(r=0.85\) are illustrative types.
\(S^{\mathrm{calm}}\) is the same model with climatology harvest and
\(\tau\equiv 0\). Players share those types; only the shocked exporter
gets the synthetic harvest multiplier.

Nested year-open-loop grid, for the record (not the headline). For the
shocked exporter, over \(\tau\in\{0,0.3,0.6,0.9\}\),
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:W}
W(\tau)=\sum_{t\in T} p_t X_{i,t}
\;-\;
\alpha\max\bigl(0,\, s^{\mathrm{gov}}_i-\overline{S}_{i,T}\bigr)^2.
\end{equation}
Calm \(\Rightarrow \tau^\star=0\); shock \(\Rightarrow \tau^\star=0.6\).
That intensity is what \(\tau^{\mathrm{on}}\) copies. World \(p\) does not
enter the penalty.
