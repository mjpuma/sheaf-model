\begin{abstract}
Gate~0 locked a 24-step wheat market with historical export cuts taken from a
diary. Gate~1 turned on substitution and left that diary in place. This note
asks whether \emph{two} exporters, sharing one slow type, can choose
state-contingent cuts \(\tau_t\) on that same spine so that a harvest failure
at one of them produces a restriction at a neighbour who did not lose harvest,
while climatology keeps both open. That is Headey's cascade in miniature
\citep{headey2011}. Types do not flip every fortnight; actions do. Knobs are
illustrative. AMIS is off. Substitution is off. Gate~0 and Gate~1 are not
re-run. The cascade that appears is \textbf{harvest diversion}, not
sequential ban-on-ban best response: Kazakhstan's open-path stock ratio is
already below the trigger before Russia's \(\tau_t\) is applied, and applying
those cuts \emph{raises} Kazakhstan's trough rather than lowering it. Ukraine
is on the market and does not play. Choropleths of who plays, how many
fortnights they are on, and open-path \(S/S^{\mathrm{calm}}\) are part of the
regular score, not an afterthought.
\end{abstract}

\section{What you are assessing}
\label{sec:scope}

This is not a new manuscript name, and it is not ``who banned in 2008.''
It is a mechanism check. The question, in one sentence:

\begin{quote}
Can two exporters, sharing the same slow type, stay open in a normal year
and can a harvest shortfall at one of them turn the \emph{other} one on,
on Headey's two-week clock?
\end{quote}

Three objects in \citet{headey2011} sit on dates, not marketing years:
export restrictions, import surges, and stock-release \emph{announcements}.
This beta implements only the first, and only as a climatology-relative
stock gate. The second and third are listed under ``not yet''
(\S\ref{sec:decide}). Characteristic government types (how much a government
cares about domestic food) can be sticky. The decision is not. Conflating
them into one annual tax is what the leftover host in
\texttt{sheaf/core.py} still does. That annual Nash is a TWIST-era prototype
\citep{schewe2017,takayama1971,samuelson1952}. It is not run here.

\paragraph{What is switched on.}
State-contingent \(\tau_{i,t}\) for Russia and Kazakhstan on the Gate~0
wheat spine. A synthetic Russia harvest \(\times 0.50\) in 2007.
Illustrative types: buffer, intensity, trigger.

\paragraph{What stays off.}
Substitution. AMIS. Estimation on 2008/10. A split domestic versus export
ask. Sequential fortnight Nash. Importer panic procurement. Japan-style
announcements. Gate~0 \texttt{CropParams} and Gate~1 \(\sigma\) are not
touched.

\paragraph{Maps.}
Every score of this layer draws choropleths of the node set
(\S\ref{sec:maps}). If a later score has no map of \emph{who} moved and
\emph{what state} moved them, it is incomplete.

\section{Clock}
\label{sec:clock}

Headey's events have dates. Vietnam and India restrict rice in October 2007;
Egypt and China in January 2008; India/Vietnam/Cambodia tighten in March;
Thailand's new government \emph{discusses} a ban that same month; Japan is
allowed to re-export rice stocks in May (the grain reportedly never ships;
the announcement is the move). Import surges sit on the same clock
\citep{headey2011,martin2012,wright2011}.

Split it:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{llp{0.52\textwidth}}
\toprule
Object & Clock & What it is \\
\midrule
Type & years / regimes & Who plays, \(\varphi\), \(\tau^{\mathrm{on}}\),
trigger \(r\), food-security weight. Moves with elections, not fortnights. \\
Action & fortnights & \(\tau_{i,t}\) on, off, or announced. Responds to
stocks relative to a normal year at the same calendar step. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Agrimate takes the restriction schedule as given~\citep{kuhla2025}. SHEAF's
destination is to let governments choose. Gate~0 first isolated that
contribution with AMIS prescribed~\citep{amis_oecd}. This beta is the first
time the \emph{choice} sits on Agrimate's 24-step clock.

\section{Host (and why bans are not contagion here)}
\label{sec:host}

The market is Gate~0 wheat: 24 steps/year, Armington on FAOSTAT \(E_0\),
ask-dominated world price, withheld grain stays as stock. There is
\textbf{one} world/ask price per crop. A ban does not create a cheaper
domestic CPI. World \(p\) does not enter the food-security term --- a
world-price penalty would punish the government for the spike the ban
itself causes.

That host limitation is why the cascade below is harvest diversion.
Demand that Russia no longer harvests spills onto neighbours
\emph{before} anyone cuts. Russia's subsequent \(\tau_t\) holds grain as
stock and slightly \emph{relieves} the neighbour. Ban-on-ban iterated best
response would need a different state variable, or a split ask. Do not
pretend this beta computed that.

\section{Scenario and knobs}
\label{sec:knobs}

\input{tables/knobs}

Players share a type. Only Russia loses harvest. Kazakhstan is the neighbour
who fires on this host because the prototype stock path is leaner, not
because 2007/08 ``chose Kazakhstan.'' Ukraine is on the market and does
not play: a Ukraine-only harvest \(\times 0.50\) does not lean Russia
enough to cross \(r\) (Russia is fat), and Russia's harvest does not push
Ukraine's open min ratio under \(r\) (0.86 versus 0.85). Who fires is who
is lean. Disclose that.

\section{What the bars establish, and what they do not}
\label{sec:claim}

\paragraph{Can claim.}
On this host, with these illustrative types: climatology keeps both
exporters open; a Russia-only harvest \(\times 0.50\) turns Russia on for
half the year and Kazakhstan on for a quarter, with no Kazakh harvest cut;
the neighbour lags or ties; the neighbour's gate is the open-path stock
ratio (harvest diversion).

\paragraph{Must disclose.}
Knobs were locked after a qualitative calm/shock flip was visible, not
estimated. The cascade is not ban contagion: closed-path min ratio for
Kazakhstan is 0.81 against open 0.76. Who fires is who is lean (Kazakhstan),
not who Headey names next (Ukraine's open min is 0.86). Russia's
Headey-path shipment cut is 0.02~MMT. One world price; withheld grain is
stock; no cheaper domestic CPI. Dual host versus annual
\texttt{core.py} welfare (consumer surplus at node prices).

\paragraph{Must not claim.}
That this is who restricted in 2007/08 or 2010/11. That types are
identified. That a Nash among many exporters was computed. That substitution
spilled (it is off). That Gate~0 leftovers were patched. That a third
exporter, or an announcement, is already in.

\section{What to decide}
\label{sec:decide}

Mark these. They are the assessment, not a backlog of papers.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Is harvest diversion enough of Headey's cascade?}
      If the scientific object is pressure on remaining Black Sea exporters
      after a harvest fails, this beta has it. If the object is ban-on-ban
      contagion, this host cannot do it without a market change (split ask,
      or a state other than \(S/S^{\mathrm{calm}}\)). Adding a third player
      will not create contagion here.
\item \textbf{Is a 0.02~MMT Headey-path bite a problem?}
      The bar is a sign check. A crisis narrative needs a bigger action, a
      different intensity rule, or honesty that year-open-loop \(\tau\) is
      doing the shipment work.
\item \textbf{Is ``who is lean'' the right neighbour?}
      Kazakhstan fires; Ukraine does not. Fine for a mechanism check. Fatal
      if the player list is treated as history.
\item \textbf{Same type for both players: enough?}
      Headey's governments did not share a personality. Heterogeneous types
      are a later knob, not a prerequisite for this check.
\item \textbf{Next development, if any?}
      An importer scramble or a stock \emph{announcement}
      \citep{headey2011}. Still synthetic. Still not estimated on 2008.
      Zip-and-upload of Gate~0 and Gate~1 is the writing next step, if any.
\end{enumerate}

Do not: retune \texttt{CropParams} or \(\rho\); densify \(\sigma\); unpause
\texttt{sheaf/dynamic\_grains.py}; graft annual Slutsky \(M_i\) onto the
spine; put the crisis game back in \texttt{core.py}; re-run Gate~0 or
Gate~1.
