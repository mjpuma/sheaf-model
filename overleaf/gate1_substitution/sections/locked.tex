\subsection{Frozen Gate~0 pieces}

Own-price elasticities, stock-to-use targets, ask and warehouse knobs, the
AMIS map, LOWESS harvests, mean food/feed, US maize industrial/ethanol,
FAOSTAT trade shares, and the calm-weather twin. Official scoring split:

stay leftovers.

\subsection{Demand host}

Gate~1 is \textbf{not} the annual linear Slutsky system
\(D=a-Mp\) in \texttt{sheaf/core.py}. That host uses
\texttt{calibration.OWN\_ELAST}. The crisis spine uses isoelastic demand
with Gate~0 \texttt{CropParams.elast} (wheat \(-0.15\), rice \(-0.20\),
maize \(-0.25\)). Switching own-price \(\varepsilon\) to
\texttt{OWN\_ELAST} would break the \(\sigma=0\) identity. Cross-price
exponents \(\eta_{gh}=\sigma\,\rho_{gh}\,|\varepsilon_g|\) are a
constant-elasticity overlay; the demand Jacobian is not
Slutsky-symmetric. Industrial/ethanol does not substitute. Disclose the
dual host in any P3 prose; do not equate Gate~1 \(\eta\) with annual
\(M_i\).

\(\rho\) is the illustrative matrix in \texttt{sheaf/calibration.py}
(wheat--maize \(0.40\) via feed; wheat--rice \(0.30\); rice--maize
\(0.20\)). It stays frozen.

\subsection{Identification}

\(\sigma\in\{0,0.3,0.6\}\) is a \textbf{pre-declared sensitivity band}, not
an estimate. Zero is Gate~0 identity. \(0.6\) is the old prototype /
\texttt{build\_countries(substitution=True)} default, not a fitted peak.
\(0.3\) is the midpoint. Do not add \(0.9\) after seeing prices. Do not
take \(\arg\max\) of 2008 \(\Delta\)corr.

\textbf{Preferred claim:} report all three. No point estimate.

\textbf{If a preferred \(\sigma\) is required:} select only on the 2007/08
\textbf{wheat\(\to\)maize} margin. Hold out rice 2008, plus 2010/11, plus
2022. Training on rice 2008 is withdrawn: that episode is an own-ban, not
a clean substitution fingerprint.

Fitting 2008 then claiming validation includes: maximizing 2007/08
rice/maize fit then reporting that window as the result; joint \(\sigma\)
on 2008+2010+2022; retuning \texttt{CropParams} / \(\rho\) / Gate~0
leftovers to help spillovers; peeking at coupled scores then declaring the
grid.

\subsection{Hard bars (every reported \(\sigma>0\))}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Identity.} Coupled \(\sigma=0\), official split, 2006--11:
      monthly world price of each grain matches the corresponding
      \texttt{run\_crop\_dynamics} to relative error \(\lesssim 0.5\%\).
      Implementation: step-level price, stocks, use, and offers at
      \(10^{-9}\) (\texttt{assert\_subst0\_matches\_gate0}; red-team RT3).
\item \textbf{Spillover sign.} In 2007/08, \(\sigma>0\) must not
      \emph{lower} rice and maize hikes relative to \(\sigma=0\) while
      wheat is up. A fall is a bug or a wrong \(\rho\) sign.
\item \textbf{Wheat Gate~0 not wrecked.} 2007/08 stays restriction-led and
      2010/11 stays production-led (isolated-leg signs). Amplitude may
      move; a sign flip is a fail.
\item \textbf{Rice still has its own ban.} Isolated rice AMIS must still
      carry most of the 2008 rice hike. Substitution is a spillover, not a
      replacement for India/Vietnam \(\tau\).
\end{enumerate}

Soft \(\Delta\)corr / \(\Delta\)hike on hold-outs are reported, not used
to pick \(\sigma^\star\). A pass must not invent a rice hike in 2010/11
(observed ratio \(<1\)).
