\section{Plain English: what we are doing}
\label{sec:plain}

Agrimate runs wheat, rice, and maize as three separate worlds
\citep{kuhla2025}. If wheat gets expensive, Agrimate's rice consumers do
not notice. SHEAF's extra claim (paper P3) is that they \emph{do}: food and
feed can switch among grains, so a wheat shock should spill into rice and
maize prices.

Gate~0 already showed that each grain's own market---harvests, stocks,
selling prices (asks), who-trades-with-whom, and the historical ban
diary---can move monthly world prices in the same direction as the World
Bank Pink Sheet~\citep{worldbank_pink,amis_oecd}. That spine is locked.
We do not retune it to make substitution look better.

Gate~1 turns on only the switching. Each grain still has its own trade
network and its own selling prices. The only new link is demand: when
wheat is dear, countries want a bit more rice and maize (food and feed),
not more ethanol. Then those three markets clear, then prices update, then
we repeat. A knob \(\sigma\) scales how strongly that switching is. We
report a small pre-declared set of knobs, not a best-fit number cooked on
2008.

\paragraph{What would count as success.}
Substitution should not break Gate~0. When wheat spikes in 2007/08, rice
and maize prices should not \emph{fall} relative to the no-switching run.
Wheat's own story (bans in 2008, harvest in 2010) should keep the same
sign. Rice's 2008 explosion should still be mostly India's and Vietnam's
bans, not ``because wheat.'' Matching the rice spike is not a substitution
win: history had a rice-specific panic. The cleanest cross-grain test in
the data is maize. 2010/11 is a contrast year: wheat and maize rose, rice
fell. Inventing a rice co-spike that year would be a miss.

\paragraph{What would be cheating.}
Choosing \(\sigma\) so 2008 rice and maize both look prettier, then calling
that same 2008 window ``validation.'' Fitting all three crises at once.
Turning the ethanol leftover into a substitute. Quietly changing Gate~0
elasticities or warehouse knobs. Starting the government export game.
\citep{schewe2017} is the storage lineage; it is not a license to add
crisis dummies.
