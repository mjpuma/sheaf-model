\begin{abstract}
This is the to-do list for paper P3, not the paper. The draft is
\texttt{overleaf/gate1\_whitepaper/}. Gate~0 (one crop at a
time, historical export bans, no switching among grains) is locked.
Agrimate cannot do substitution~\citep{kuhla2025}. SHEAF can, on that
spine, by letting food and feed respond to the other grains' prices. We
do not fit a favourite \(\sigma\) on 2008. We report a pre-declared band,
keep Gate~0 parameters frozen, and write down the next experiment before
running it. Edit the coloured box, the log, and \S\ref{sec:next} after
each sitting so the next sitting starts from the truth.
\end{abstract}

\section{E5: maize 2010 hike collapse}
\label{sec:e5}

Cheap rice is not the main channel. Zero wheat--maize \(\rho\) restores
87\% of the hike-ratio drop; holding rice on the \(\sigma=0\) path restores
2\%. The February 2011 maize \emph{level} is slightly higher under
\(\sigma=0.6\). The ratio falls because June 2009 maize is \$182 at
\(\sigma=0\) and \$283 at \(\sigma=0.6\)---wheat--maize feed substitution
keeps maize expensive after 2008. Disclose this; do not retune \(\rho\) to
pretty-up the 2010 hike statistic.

\section{E6: 2021--23 Ukraine-war hold-out}
\label{sec:e6}

Same flags as Gate~0 Ukraine. Identity at \(\sigma=0\) holds. Wheat stays
restriction-led. Maize stays flat (\(\times 1.01\)). Rice drifts up
(\(\times 0.99\to\times 1.18\) vs observed \(\times 0.91\))---a soft
wrong-way miss, not a 2008-scale spike. Wheat monthly corr rises
(\(+0.69\to +0.75\)); that is \textbf{not} a reason to pick
\(\sigma^\star\).
