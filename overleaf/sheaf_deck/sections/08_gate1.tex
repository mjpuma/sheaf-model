\section{Gate 1 --- isoelastic substitution}

\begin{frame}[label=gate1]{Dual demand host --- do not graft $M_i$ onto the spine}
\topic{Substitution lives on the 24-step market, not on the old annual demand matrix.}
\matchbanner{GATE1\_PLAN.md: Gate 1 is not README \S1}{dynamic\_coupled.cross\_price\_eta}

README \S1 is a linear Slutsky system $D_i=a_i-M_i p_i$ on the
\textbf{annual leftover}. Gate~1 does not use it.

Gate~1 multiplies Gate~0 flex demand by a Jacobi factor built from all
three world prices. Industrial/ethanol use still does not substitute.
\texttt{CropParams} stay locked. $\sigma\in\{0,0.3,0.6\}$ is a
pre-declared band, not an estimate. There is no $\sigma^\star$.

\symsep $\sigma^\star$ would be a \emph{selected} value --- none is claimed.}

{$\sigma$ dimensionless. $\eta_{gh}$ dimensionless (elasticities).}

\begin{frame}{Jacobi step --- and why it needs no inner solver}
\topic{Each step, all three prices scale food demand once; then each crop clears exactly as in Gate 0.}
\matchbanner{dynamic\_coupled.\_simulate\_coupled}{dynamic\_coupled.py:112--217}

Each step $t$:

\begin{enumerate}
\item $\mathrm{fac}_t = \exp\bigl(\eta \log(p_t/p_0)\bigr)$, using all
      three world prices from the \emph{start} of the step.
\item Each grain $g$ runs the Gate~0 market with
      $d^g = C^{\mathrm{flex},g}\cdot\mathrm{fac}^g_t + C^{\mathrm{ind},g}$.
\item Each grain updates its own price $p^g$ exactly as in Gate~0.
\end{enumerate}

Networks, AMIS diaries, harvests, warehouses, and asks all stay per-crop.
Coupling runs \textbf{only} through flex demand. This is Jacobi, not
Gauss--Seidel: within a step rice does not see an already-updated wheat
price, so there is no inner fixed point to converge and no tolerance to
set --- one extra multiply per step over Gate~0.
\syms{$g$ grain (wheat, rice, maize) \symsep $\mathrm{fac}^g_t$ multiplicative
demand scale for grain $g$ (dimensionless; $1$ means no substitution effect)
\symsep $\eta$ the $3\times3$ matrix of own- and cross-price elasticities
(dimensionless) \symsep $p_t$ the three world prices at the start of the step
and $p_0$ their references (real 2010 \$/t) \symsep $d^g$ desired use,
$C^{\mathrm{flex},g}$ food/feed, $C^{\mathrm{ind},g}$ industrial (all MMT/step).}
\bubbles{Jacobi is a numerical protocol, not a parameter.}
{fac dimensionless; $p$ real 2010 \$/t.}
{\texttt{dynamic\_coupled.py:113--114, 121--122}.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Identity: $\sigma=0$ recovers Gate~0}
\topic{Turning substitution off recovers the three separate Gate~0 price paths.}
\matchbanner{README \S6 spirit; GATE1 hard bar 1}{dynamic\_coupled.assert\_subst0\_matches\_gate0}

Coupled $\sigma=0$ monthly prices vs three independent
\texttt{run\_crop\_dynamics} paths:

\[
\max \frac{|p^{\mathrm{coupled}}-p^{\mathrm{solo}}|}{\max(|p^{\mathrm{solo}}|,1)}
= 4.3\times 10^{-16}
\]

across wheat, rice, and maize (the pass bar is $0.5\%$). This is the
Gate~1 analogue of README \S6's proposition, demonstrated on the
\textbf{live} host rather than on the annual QP.
\syms{$p^{\mathrm{coupled}}$ monthly world price from the three-crop coupled run
and $p^{\mathrm{solo}}$ from three separate single-crop runs (both real 2010
\$/t) \symsep $\sigma$ cross-grain substitution scale; $\sigma=0$ zeroes every
off-diagonal $\eta_{gh}$ (dimensionless) \symsep the max is taken over months
and over the three grains.}
\bubbles{No parameter. This is an identity assert.}
{Relative price error, dimensionless.}
{\texttt{verify\_claims.py} section 10; \texttt{assert\_subst0\_matches\_gate0}.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What Gate 1 does not claim}
\topic{What Gate~1 does not claim: no fitted substitution, no export game, no country-level CES map.}
\begin{itemize}
\item It does not estimate $\sigma$.
\item It does not turn on the export game.
\item It does not fix Gate~0 consumption leftovers.
\item It does not produce a spatial substitution fingerprint at the
      country level that looks like Agrimate's cross-region plots ---
      there is one world price, so $\Delta C$ from substitution is nearly
      uniform in $p$.
\item 2022 rice drifts the wrong way ($\times 0.99\to\times 1.18$ vs obs
      $\times 0.91$). Disclosed. Not a retune target.
\end{itemize}
\syms{$\sigma$ substitution scale (dimensionless). $\Delta C$ consumption change (MMT). $p$ one world price per crop (2010 \$/t).}
\end{frame}
