\section{Lineage and destination}

\begin{frame}[label=lineage]{TWIST $\to$ Agrimate $\to$ SHEAF}
\topic{Where SHEAF sits: after TWIST and Agrimate, and what it adds.}
\matchbanner{README lineage paragraph}{README.md:490--495}

TWIST \citep{schewe2017} --- applied in \citet{falkendal2021} ---
reproduces annual world wheat prices from a stylised price--supply curve.
It does not resolve a trade network. It does not take export restrictions
as a first-order object.

Agrimate \citep{kuhla2025} adds a dynamic agent-based network with
commercial and strategic stockholding, 24 steps per year, and hindcasts
2007/08 and 2010/11. Export restrictions enter as an \textbf{exogenous
AMIS schedule}. Both predecessors are \textbf{single-commodity}.

SHEAF exists to put two missing pieces on that network:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Endogenous strategy} --- governments choose restrictions.
\item \textbf{Cross-grain substitution} --- wheat, rice, maize linked on demand.
\end{enumerate}
\syms{AMIS the FAO-hosted Agricultural Market Information System policy diary
\symsep ``exogenous'' means the restriction is read from that diary
\symsep ``endogenous'' means the model chooses it \symsep single-commodity:
one grain, no cross-price link \symsep 24 steps per year $=$ half-month
fortnights.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What SHEAF is \emph{not} claiming}
\topic{What we are not claiming --- design choices, not bugs.}
SHEAF is Agrimate-\emph{aligned}, not an Agrimate clone and not a
Deaton--Laroque storage equilibrium \citep{deaton1992,wright1982,williams1991}.

\begin{itemize}
\item No rational-expectations competitive storage; no endogenous acreage.
\item No WASDE ethanol gallon series (USA maize uses a PSD FSI residual).
\item No flour / bread / kcal wheat-equivalent. Unprocessed grain, as
      Agrimate \citep{kuhla2025}. Rice is milled; wheat is not.
\item No oligopolistic traders as strategic players (the market layer
      \emph{is} traders arbitraging; the state sets the ban).
\item Spatial price equilibrium of Enke--Samuelson--Takayama--Judge
      \citep{enke1951,samuelson1952,takayama1971} lives in the
      \textbf{annual leftover}, not in the 24-step ask market.
\end{itemize}

Those are design choices (\textbf{E}), disclosed. They are not bugs.
\syms{Category \textbf{E} in the \texttt{CLAUDE.md} scheme: a difference of
modelling philosophy, not a mathematical error (\textbf{A}) or a coding bug
(\textbf{B}) \symsep rational-expectations competitive storage: the
Deaton--Laroque class, where storers forecast the equilibrium price
distribution \symsep endogenous acreage: planted area responding to price.}
\end{frame}
