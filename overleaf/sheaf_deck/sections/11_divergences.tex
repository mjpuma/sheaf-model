\begin{frame}[label=questions]{Questions for discussion}
\topic{Five questions for the Agrimate authors. Not whether this is Deaton--Laroque --- it is not claimed to be.}
The comparison that matters is Agrimate, not Deaton--Laroque.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Is the 24-step ask-dominated market a fair cousin of Agrimate's
      commercial layer, or a different object we should name more
      carefully? (Method of solution is its own jump.)
\item Is an offer-cut $\tau$ the right translation of AMIS, versus
      Agrimate's edge shutdown?
\item Is Jacobi isoelastic $\eta$ the substitution you would put on this
      spine, versus a nested CES / AIDS at the country?
\item Is climatology-relative $S/S^{\mathrm{calm}}$ the right Headey
      trigger, or do you want importer procurement and announcements
      before any who-restricts score?
\item Is an explicit ask/Armington map the right cousin of Agrimate's
      per-step supplier / consumer / purchaser optimizations, or a
      different object we should name more carefully?
\end{enumerate}
\syms{$\tau_{i,t}$ export-offer cut, a fraction in $[0,1]$, not a \$/t tax
\symsep $\eta_{gh}$ isoelastic cross-grain demand exponents (dimensionless)
\symsep $S^{\mathrm{open}}/S^{\mathrm{calm}}$ shocked-open stocks over
climatology stocks at the same step (dimensionless) \symsep CES constant
elasticity of substitution; AIDS almost-ideal demand system \symsep
``ask'' $q_{i,t}$ an exporter's offer price (real 2010 \$/t).}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{One-sentence summary}
\topic{One sentence: what the crisis model is, and what the leftover annual code is not.}
\hyperlink{sitmap}{\beamerreturnbutton{sitting map}}
\vspace{0.3em}
SHEAF's crisis model is a 24-step, ask-dominated, single-world-price
market with AMIS offer cuts (Gate~0), Jacobi isoelastic cross-grain
demand on that locked spine (Gate~1, no $\sigma^\star$), and a
Headey-clock climatology-relative $\tau_t$ whose two-player cascade is
harvest diversion (Gate~2 beta). The annual SPE plus year-Nash in
\texttt{core.py} is still in the repository. It is the TWIST leftover,
not the 2007/08 crisis host.
\syms{24-step clock: $T_y=24$ steps per calendar year, $\Delta t\approx15.2$
days \symsep ``ask-dominated'': the world price weights exporter asks
$q_{i,t}$ (real 2010 \$/t) with weight $\omega$ and a scarcity term with
$1-\omega$ \symsep $\tau_t$ export-offer cut (fraction in $[0,1]$)
\symsep $\sigma$ cross-grain substitution scale (dimensionless); no
$\sigma^\star$ is selected \symsep SPE spatial price equilibrium.}
\end{frame}
