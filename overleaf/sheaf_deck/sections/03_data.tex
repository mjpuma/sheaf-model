\begin{frame}{USDA PSD --- production, consumption, stocks}

Q_{i,y},\; C_{i,y},\; R_{i,y}^{\mathrm{end}} \quad\text{in MMT}.
\]

(\texttt{dynamic\_crop.\_psd\_annual:250--254}).
\bubbles{No free parameter. RoW is an accounting identity.}
{1000~MT raw $\to$ MMT.}
{USDA FAS PSD Online, commodities Wheat / Rice, Milled / Corn.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Do not score consumption on \texttt{load\_crop\_world}}
The world-aggregate CSVs in \texttt{data/usda\_world/} omit the EU as a
node and are a different USDA aggregate. Official P1 consumption bars use
the \textbf{country-sum} of \texttt{load\_psd\_country}, not
\texttt{load\_crop\_world} (README \S8; \texttt{GATE0\_PARAMETERIZATION.md}
\S6).

World-aggregate series remain useful for a long LOWESS history
(1960--2025) and for \texttt{crisis\_forcing} on the leftover annual host.
\bubbles{Scoring choice is structural, not a knob.}
{MMT.}
{\texttt{data/usda\_world/}: AgRichter/PSD, refreshed 2026-01-21.
\texttt{data/usda\_psd/}: FAS bulk 2026-08-21.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{FAOSTAT E0 is a pattern, not a trade volume}
\divbanner{README table can be read as ``E0 is tonnes''}{data\_faostat.py:21--23}

\textbf{Trade volume is MMT/step}, from USDA offers $O$ and demand $D$.
FAOSTAT E0 is never added up as tonnes. The vendored wheat 2006--07
matrix sums to $4.786\times 10^{14}$ --- not $\sim 120$~MMT of world
wheat trade. So the code keeps only shares (units cancel):

{\small
\[
A_{ij} = \frac{E_{ij}}{\sum_k E_{ik}}, \quad
S_{ij} = \frac{E_{ij}}{\sum_k E_{kj}}, \quad A_{ii}=S_{ii}=0,
\]
\[
\mathrm{ship}_{ij}
= \min\bigl(O_{i,t}\tilde A_{ij},\, D_{j,t} S_{ij}\bigr).
\]}

Egypt wheat 2006--07 sources: Russia $37\%$, USA $29\%$, EU $10\%$.
That mix is what E0 contributes. The tonnes on the link come from $O,D$.
\bubbles{$A$, $S$ structural. No E0 scale on the spine.}
{Shares dimensionless. $\mathrm{ship}$, $O$, $D$: MMT/step.}
{FoodTradeNetwork E0, 2006--2007 Avg. \texttt{\_bilateral\_clear}.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Vietnam rice E0 is a documented data repair}
\matchbanner{GATE0\_PARAMETERIZATION \S2.4}{dynamic\_crop.py:439--465}

Crisis-window rice files (2006--07, 2010--11) have a \textbf{zero Vietnam
export row}. The code copies Vietnam's destination mix from 2019--21 and
scales the row to 12\% of that crisis-window matrix sum (order-of-magnitude
world-rice-export share).

This is a hole in the vendored E0, not a 2008 price knob. After repair,
Vietnam's $A$ row sums to 1. Verified.
\bubbles{\texttt{\_VNM\_RICE\_EXPORT\_SHARE = 0.12} is a data repair,
labelled as such. Not fit to 2008.}
{Dimensionless shares.}
{FAOSTAT rice E0 2006--07 (hole) + 2019--21 mix.
\texttt{dynamic\_crop.py:439--465}.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The leftover AMIS map is a different object}
\leftoverbanner{data\_usda.amis\_tau\_schedule}{data\_usda.py:351--373}

The same AMIS file is also mapped to \textbf{\$/t wedges} for the annual
SPE leftover (\texttt{\_AMIS\_TAU\_AGRIMATE}: ban $=500$, tax $=150$, \ldots).
That mapping is what \texttt{LEVEL1\_INTERROGATION.md} used. It is
\textbf{not} applied by \texttt{dynamic\_crop.amis\_export\_cuts}.

If you see $\tau=500$ in a conversation about SHEAF, you are looking at
the leftover host.
\bubbles{Two maps, one diary. Spine = fraction. Leftover = \$/t.}
{Spine: $[0,1]$. Leftover: \$/t.}
{\texttt{dynamic\_crop.\_AMIS\_CUT} vs \texttt{data\_usda.\_AMIS\_TAU\_AGRIMATE}.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Crop calendars}
\matchbanner{README \S8 harvest calendars}{seasonal.py:44--65, data/crop\_calendars/}

Annual production is spread with \textbf{triangular} month weights inside
each country's harvest window, then split equally across the two
half-months. Weights sum to 1 (verified, max $|{\sum w-1}|=2\times10^{-16}$).

Nodes with negligible production still carry a placeholder window (e.g.\
Thailand wheat). Missing calendar row $\to$ uniform 24-step weights.
\bubbles{Windows are structural. Shape is triangular, not uniform ---
Agrimate-style concentration mid-season.}
{Dimensionless shares of annual MMT.}
{Hand-curated from USDA FAS crop-calendar charts.
\texttt{data/crop\_calendars/PROVENANCE.txt}. Prototype, not a SAGE grid.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Marketing-year end months}
PSD ending stocks are \textbf{local marketing-year} carry, not calendar
December. Scoring world wheat in May and maize in August is the USDA FAS
convention, not Agrimate's 28-region grouping.

\texttt{sheaf/marketing\_years.py} stores per-node overrides (India wheat
MY-end = March; Argentina wheat = November; China maize = September).
Unlisted nodes fall back to the crop default.
\bubbles{Scoring month is structural. Not a tightness knob.}
{Calendar month $1$--$12$.}
{USDA FAS \emph{Grain: World Markets and Trade} convention.
\texttt{marketing\_years.py:17--68}.}
\end{frame}
