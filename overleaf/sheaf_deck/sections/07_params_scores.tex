retune list.

\item Wheat 2010 hike ratio is production-led after LOWESS.
\item Wheat 2006/07 still $\times 1.46$ vs obs $\sim\times 0.95$.
\item Maize 2010/11 a bit high ($\times 1.70$ vs $\times 1.44$).
\item Country stock \emph{levels}: world MY-end matches; many exporters sit
      on the global safety floor. $\Delta$stock signs $\approx$ coin-flip.
\item World ex-China maize $\times 1.49$ (China MY is September, world bar
      is August).
\item Consumption path is flat/down (mean flex $+$ $\varepsilon<0$) while
      PSD use rises. Wheat corr $-0.17$, maize $-0.64$, rice $-0.74$.
\item $\tau$ cuts \textbf{offers}; shipments are demand-constrained.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
