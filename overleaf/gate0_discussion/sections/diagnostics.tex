\section{Diagnostics that would distinguish the alternatives}
\label{sec:diagnostics}

Each item below is a measurement on the current recursion, or on a
labelled experiment that does not retune the 2007/08 window. They are
stated so that an alternative can be rejected or accepted on evidence
rather than on preference.

\paragraph{Properties that must already hold.}
Four are exact and should be asserted in tests. When the crisis run
matches the twin in accessible stocks, unmet import demand, and blockage,
\eqref{eq:pscar}--\eqref{eq:pstar} return \(p^\star_t=p_0\). Maize export
restrictions applied in isolation must not lower the world price; this is
the condition that set \(\alpha_r\) in \eqref{eq:ask}. Availability is
exhausted by consumption, net trade, the change in stocks, and the
capacity drawdown in \eqref{eq:stocks}. The twin holds price at \(p_0\),
so it exerts no feedback through \eqref{eq:demand}.

\paragraph{Contemporaneous demand (option B).}
Along a scored trajectory, recompute \eqref{eq:demand} at the price
\(p_t\) that the period actually produced, and compare it to the \(d_{i,t}\)
that entered \eqref{eq:offers}. The distribution of
\(d_{i,t}(p_t)-d_{i,t}(p_{t-1})\) bounds what contemporaneous consistency
can do. If it is small, the fixed point changes labels rather than
results; if it is large in 2007/08, the informational structure genuinely
differs and the change is substantive.

\paragraph{Offer prices as a markup rule.}
Regress \(\log(q_{i,t}/q_{i,t-1})\) on the fill rate \(f_{i,t}\) from
\eqref{eq:ask}. The current specification is exponential in
\(f_{i,t}-\theta\); a markup over marginal cost implies a different
relationship, in particular a dependence on cost rather than on excess
demand. Establish whether the existing rule is already observationally
close to a markup before replacing it.

\paragraph{Storage as intertemporal arbitrage.}
Under an arbitrage condition, offers should rise with the expected price
increase. Under \eqref{eq:offers} they rise with surplus above
\(T_{i,t}\). Scatter \(O_{i,t}\) against \(p_{t+1}-p_t\) as a descriptive
baseline, recognising that this is not decisive because cover already
covaries with the harvest calendar. The failed experiment with
country-specific \(\sigma^{\mathrm{stu}}_i\) (Chinese maize stocks
inflating threefold) is the relevant prior.

\paragraph{Domestic price insulation.}
Inspect the state directly: no variable distinct from \(p_t\) enters
\eqref{eq:demand} for a restricting country. Any claim about consumer
welfare inside a banning country is therefore outside what Gate~0
currently represents \citep{martin2012}.

\paragraph{National versus world lean-season clocks.}
Compare the world harvest-pulse horizon in \(L_{i,t}\) against each
country's own harvest calendar. Cover held in the month preceding a
country's own harvest is a direct signature of the common world clock.

\paragraph{Channel ablation.}
Re-run the official harvest-plus-restrictions score with, in turn,
\(\omega=1\) (offer prices only), \(\omega=0\) (scarcity only),
\(\alpha_r=0\) (no rival markup), and \(\kappa_b=0\) (no blockage term).
This attributes 2007/08 and 2010/11 to specific channels without
introducing a new specification.

\paragraph{Known residuals under the present rule.}
These stand regardless of which option is chosen, and should not be used
to motivate a change unless the change targets their mechanism. Wheat
2007/08 remains high and restriction-led. World consumption does not track
the USDA path, which is expected given that official scores use mean food
and feed demand and \eqref{eq:demand} reduces consumption as price rises.
Many exporters sit at the safety-stock floor \(s_i\). Restrictions cut
offers substantially more than shipments. Introducing an optimisation
under the world price does not automatically resolve any of these, and
fitting \(\kappa_b\) to 2007/08 would not constitute standing behind the
market.
