\section{Purpose and scope}
\label{sec:howto}

SHEAF is intended as a model in which governments choose export
restrictions endogenously and consumers substitute across grains. Neither
mechanism is the subject of this note. Before they are added, each crop's
market must be defensible in isolation: prices must move in the correct
months, stocks must be compared at the month USDA reports them, and
historically observed restrictions must reduce export offers in the
countries that imposed them. That prior claim is Gate~0.

Snapshot scores for 2006--11 exist. They are not treated as settled.
Following the sitting with the Agrimate authors \citep{kuhla2025}, the
question is whether the \emph{objects underlying} those scores are ones we
are prepared to publish. If they are not, adding cross-commodity
substitution and an endogenous restriction game on top leaves the
foundation weak even where the correlations are acceptable.

The crisis host runs on a two-week step, 24 per year, matching Agrimate's
clock. An annual spatial-equilibrium formulation remains in the repository
but is parked; it is a different host, and it produced the wrong sign for
the 2007/08 wheat episode.

\paragraph{Structure.}
Section~\ref{sec:notation} defines every symbol with its unit and
parameter class. Section~\ref{sec:dynamics} is the substance: the two-week
recursion in words, then as equations, with each symbol restated where it
appears so that the equations can be read without turning back.
Section~\ref{sec:options} takes the sitting's questions one at a time,
stating what the model does now and what each alternative would require.
Section~\ref{sec:diagnostics} gives the measurements that would settle
them, and \S\ref{sec:order} proposes an order of discussion. Four figures
are included; alternatives that modify a single equation are described in
that equation rather than redrawn.

\paragraph{The standard being applied.}
Every term used at the sitting should name a specific object in this
model, and what we write in a paper should match what the code executes.
Known residuals --- wheat 2007/08 still high, world consumption not
tracking USDA, many exporters resting on the safety-stock floor --- remain
open until a change is directed at the mechanism that generates them. The
2007/08 window is not a fitting target.
