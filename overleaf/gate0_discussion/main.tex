\begin{abstract}
This is a discussion note, not a results paper. Gate~0 is the two-week
grain market on which the rest of SHEAF rests: a single crop at a time,
historically observed export restrictions imposed exogenously, no
substitution among wheat, rice, and maize, and no endogenous policy
choice. Coauthors asked how the world price is determined, which object
``baseline'' refers to, why available grain is withheld rather than sold,
and whether the price update should derive from an optimisation problem.
We state the two-week recursion in full --- in words, then as equations
with every symbol defined at the point of use and carrying explicit units
--- and set each proposed alternative against the equation it would
replace and the measurement that would distinguish it. Four figures.
Nothing is decided here. Cross-commodity substitution and the restriction
game remain deferred until this market is one we would publish as stated.
\end{abstract}
