\section{Mathematical formulation}
\label{sec:math}

This section states every equation the Gate~0 spine actually solves. Symbols
match \texttt{sheaf/dynamic\_crop.py} as of the commit that accompanies this
note. Quantities are million tonnes (MMT) per step unless labelled annual;
prices are real dollars per tonne (World Bank Pink Sheet, MUV 2010).

\subsection{Clock, nodes, and state}

There are $T_y=24$ steps per calendar year ($\Delta t\approx 15.2$ days),
matching Agrimate~\citep{kuhla2025}. Nodes $i=1,\dots,n$ are the 17 named
SHEAF countries plus a Rest-of-World residual. One crop $g$ is run at a time.
The state at the start of step $t$ is commercial-plus-pipeline stock
$S_{i,t}$, world price $p_t$, and exporter ask $q_{i,t}$. Harvest inflow
$H_{i,t}$ arrives at the beginning of the step. Availability is
\[
\mathrm{avail}_{i,t}=S_{i,t}+H_{i,t}.
\]

\subsection{Harvest calendars and LOWESS anomalies}
\label{sec:harvest}

Annual USDA PSD production $Y_{i,y}$ is never laid on the calendar as a raw
level. The twin (climatology) path is the score-window \emph{mean} seasonal
harvest: triangular month weights from \texttt{sheaf/seasonal.py} applied to
each country's mean production over the scoring years. Treatment harvest is
that climatology times a per-country LOWESS multiplier,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:harvest}
H_{i,y}^{\mathrm{ann}}=\overline{H}_i\cdot(1+a_{i,y}),
\qquad
a_{i,y}=\frac{Y_{i,y}-\hat Y_{i,y}}{\hat Y_{i,y}},
\end{equation}
where $\hat Y$ is a local-linear LOWESS trend~\citep{cleveland1979} on a padded
PSD history with a $\sim$10-year window (\texttt{detrend\_anomalies}; tricube
weights, local intercept at the evaluation year). Signed anomalies keep
surpluses (\texttt{shock\_mode=full}).

\textbf{Why not the in-sample mean?} Post-2008 yield growth inflates a
2006--11 mean. World wheat 2006 is about $-9\%$ versus that mean and only
$-4\%$ versus LOWESS. Using raw levels treats 2006/07 as a crash (spurious
lean-season spike) and under-weights the 2010 Russian drought, which sits
near a boom-inflated mean. The same bias made 2008 maize look scarce when it
is on-trend. Agrimate uses the same LOWESS construction; we follow it.

Rice calendars are multi-crop (kharif+rabi / early+late) except Vietnam,
whose autumn pulse is kept so the 2008 ban still hits offers.

\subsection{Demand: flex versus industrial}
\label{sec:demand}

PSD domestic use is split
\begin{equation}
C_{i,y}=C^{\mathrm{flex}}_{i,y}+C^{\mathrm{ind}}_{i,y}.
\end{equation}
Flex use (food+feed) faces an isoelastic short-run response. Industrial use
is price-inelastic in the step (a mandate, not a consumer FOC). USDA
international PSD reports Feed and FSI, not ethanol gallons. For nodes in
\texttt{industrial\_nodes} (default: USA maize only),
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:industrial}
C^{\mathrm{ind}}_{i,y}=\min\Bigl(C_{i,y},\;
\max\bigl(0,\;\mathrm{FSI}_{i,y}-\overline{\mathrm{FSI}}_{i,2000\text{--}04}\bigr)\Bigr).
\end{equation}
That residual is the RFS-era FSI surge~\citep{rfs2005,abbott2011,roberts2013}:
US maize FSI 77~MMT in 2005 to 163~MMT in 2010, while feed fell. Wheat FSI is
flat in this window; rice has no FSI attribute. China's FSI trend is
\emph{not} treated as ethanol.

Official P1 uses \textbf{mean flex} over the score window plus industrial on
(\texttt{use\_demand=False}, \texttt{use\_industrial=True} for USA maize).
Year-by-year food/feed is a sensitivity, not the headline. The twin has mean
flex and $C^{\mathrm{ind}}=0$.

Within-step desired use is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:desired}
d_{i,t}=C^{\mathrm{flex}}_{i,t}\,(p_t/p_0)^{\varepsilon}+C^{\mathrm{ind}}_{i,t},
\end{equation}
with $\varepsilon<0$ (Table~\ref{tab:params}). $p_0$ is the mean real Pink
Sheet in the numeraire year (2006 for the 2006--11 hindcast; 2021 for the
Ukraine-war exercise).

\subsection{Lean foresight, targets, offers}
\label{sec:lean}

Let $h_t$ be steps to the next global harvest pulse (cumulative future world
harvest $\ge 12\%$ of mean annual world $H$, capped at 24 steps). Expected
harvest for foresight blends realized and seasonal paths,
\begin{equation}
H^{\mathrm{exp}}_{i,t}=\phi\,H_{i,t}+(1-\phi)\,H^{\mathrm{seas}}_{i,t}.
\end{equation}
Rolling sums $C^{\mathrm{ahead}}$, $H^{\mathrm{ahead}}$ cover the horizon.
Lean cover and the stock target are
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lean}
L_{i,t}=\max\bigl(0,\,C^{\mathrm{ahead}}_{i,t}+C_{i,t}-H^{\mathrm{ahead}}_{i,t}-H^{\mathrm{exp}}_{i,t}\bigr),
\qquad
T_{i,t}=L_{i,t}+s_i,
\end{equation}
with safety $s_i=\mathtt{stu\_target}\cdot C_i^{\mathrm{ann}}$. Purchase demand
and export offers are
\begin{align}
\label{eq:demand-offers}
D_{i,t}&=\max(0,d_{i,t}-\mathrm{avail}_{i,t})
+\lambda\max\bigl(0,\,T_{i,t}-\max(0,\mathrm{avail}_{i,t}-d_{i,t})\bigr),\\
O_{i,t}&=\max(0,\mathrm{avail}_{i,t}-d_{i,t}-T_{i,t})\,(1-\tau_{i,t}).
\end{align}
$\lambda$ is a partial-adjustment speed, not a storage Euler equation.
$\tau_{i,t}\in[0,1]$ is the AMIS quantity cut (\S\ref{sec:amis}).

This is Agrimate-aligned commercial cover plus an exogenous restriction,
not a rational-expectations competitive-storage equilibrium
\citep{wright1982,williams1991,deaton1992}. Continuity with TWIST/Agrimate
is the design intent~\citep{schewe2017,kuhla2025}; a departure from
Deaton--Laroque is not scored as an error.

\subsection{Warehouse capacity}
\label{sec:warehouse}

Carry capacity at step $t$ is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:warehouse}
W_{i,t}=\mathtt{max\_stu}\,C_i^{\mathrm{ann}}
+\mathtt{pipeline\_max\_steps}\cdot C_i^{\mathrm{ann}}/24
+\mathbf{1}_{\{\mathtt{pipeline}>0\}}H_{i,t}.
\end{equation}
Pulse crops (wheat/maize, \texttt{pipeline\_max\_steps=12}) pad $W$ with
same-step harvest so a pulse is not incinerated on intake. Rice
(\texttt{pipeline=0}) clips toward carry: padding $W$ with every monsoon
step stored harvest as if it were silos. Overflow is soft-clipped,
\[
S_{i,t+1}\leftarrow S_{i,t+1}-\mathtt{warehouse\_lambda}\cdot\max(0,S_{i,t+1}-W_{i,t}).
\]
A one-step hard dump incinerated $\sim$10\% of wheat/maize harvest and
starved lean-season offers. The working-stock term is a \emph{constant} cap
(not shrunk as the next harvest approaches). Seed stocks at spin-up are
clipped to $\max(\mathtt{max\_stu}\,C,\,1.5s)$; the within-step ceiling is
\texttt{max\_stu}\,C, not $1.5s$.

\subsection{AMIS quantity cuts}
\label{sec:amis}

AMIS/OECD records policy \emph{classes}, not tonnes withheld
\citep{amis_oecd,kuhla2025}. Mixed classes (\texttt{Maize + Wheat}) are
dropped. Overlapping policies on the same node take the max cut:
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\toprule
AMIS \texttt{PolicyMeasure\_Name} & $\tau$ \\
\midrule
Export prohibition & $0.95$ \\
Export quota & $0.70$ \\
Export tax & $0.50$ \\
Minimum export price & $0.35$ \\
Licensing requirement & $0.25$ \\
Restriction on customs clearance point & $0.15$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
This is Agrimate's map (ban $\approx 95\%$, tax $\approx 50\%$). It is
structural data, not a free parameter, and is not refit per crisis.
Grain withheld in Fig.~3 analogues is the model object
$O_{i,t}\tau_{i,t}/(1-\tau_{i,t})$ --- offers that would have existed
without the cut --- not FAOSTAT observed trade.

\subsection{Armington clear and adaptive asks}
\label{sec:armington}

FAOSTAT E0~\citep{faostat_trade} supplies destination shares $A_{ij}$ and
source shares $S_{ij}$ with zero diagonal (no recorded self-trade). For
2006--11 the default window is 2006--07; for 2021--23 we use 2019--21
(latest vendored vintage). FAOSTAT rice E0 in the crisis files has a
\textbf{zero Vietnam row}. \texttt{load\_trade\_shares} fills the destination
mix from 2019--21 and scales the row to 12\% of crisis-window export total
(Vietnam's observed world-rice-export share). That is a data repair, not a
2008 price knob.

Asks reweight destinations,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ask-reweight}
\tilde A_{ij}\propto A_{ij}\,(p_0/q_{i,t})^{\gamma}
\quad\text{(rows renormalised)},
\end{equation}
then preferred links clear as
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:armington}
\mathrm{ship}_{ij}=\min\bigl(O_{i,t}\tilde A_{ij},\,D_{j,t}S_{ij}\bigr).
\end{equation}
A residual pool may fill at most fraction $\nu=0.15$ of leftover demand,
allocated in proportion to residual offers. This is a reduced-form
Armington~\citep{armington1969} on a \emph{prescribed} network. Endogenous
network formation is paper P2, not Gate~0.

Fill rates update asks (sold-out $\Rightarrow$ raise; leftover $\Rightarrow$
cut), with a survivor markup when preferred sources are blocked (Agrimate's
oligopolist channel):
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ask}
q_{i,t+1}
=\bigl[(1-\beta)\,q_{i,t}\exp\bigl(\alpha(\mathrm{fill}_{i,t}-\theta)
+\alpha_r b_t\cdot\mathbf{1}_{O_{i,t}>0}\bigr)
+\beta\,p_t\bigr],
\end{equation}
clipped to $[0.45\,p_0,\,2.8\,p_0]$. Fill is shipped/$O$ (target
$\theta=0.70$ if $O=0$). $b_t$ is the preferred-source block fraction
(\S\ref{sec:price}). $\alpha_r=0$ recovers the own-fill law. Shared
$\alpha_r=0.80$ is the smallest value at which isolated maize $\tau$ does
not cut the 14-month mean world price; it is not fit to 2008 peaks.

Physical update after trade:
\begin{align}
\mathrm{cons}_{i,t}&=\min\bigl(d_{i,t},\,\max(0,\mathrm{avail}_{i,t}-\mathrm{shipped}_i+\mathrm{received}_i)\bigr),\\
S_{i,t+1}&=\max\bigl(0,\mathrm{avail}_{i,t}-\mathrm{shipped}_i-\mathrm{cons}_{i,t}+\mathrm{received}_i\bigr),
\end{align}
then the warehouse clip of \S\ref{sec:warehouse}.

\subsection{World price}
\label{sec:price}

World-accessible free stocks subtract lean need and grain locked behind
export cuts (otherwise a ban looks like abundance):
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:free}
\mathrm{locked}_t=\sum_i\tau_{i,t}\max(0,S_{i,t+1}-T_{i,t}),
\quad
\mathrm{free}_t=\sum_i S_{i,t+1}-\sum_i L_{i,t}-\mathrm{locked}_t.
\end{equation}
A path-matched \textbf{twin} is simulated once: seasonal-mean harvest, mean
flex, zero industrial, $\tau\equiv 0$, same seed stocks. Let
$F^{\mathrm{twin}}_t$ and $u^{\mathrm{twin}}_t$ be its free stocks and unmet
fractions. Unmet and blockage on the treatment path are
\begin{equation}
u_t=1-\frac{\sum_i\mathrm{received}_{i,t}}{\max(\sum_i D_{i,t},\epsilon)},
\quad
b_t=\frac{\sum_{i,j} S_{ij}\,\tau_{i,t}\,D_{j,t}}{\max(\sum_j D_{j,t},\epsilon)},
\quad
\Delta u_t=\max(0,u_t-u^{\mathrm{twin}}_t).
\end{equation}
Trade-weighted ask $p^{\mathrm{tr}}_t=\sum_i q_{i,t}\mathrm{shipped}_{i,t}/\sum_i\mathrm{shipped}_{i,t}$
(or $p_t$ if no trade). Scarcity
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:scar}
p^{\mathrm{scar}}_t=p_0\cdot r_t^{\eta}
\cdot\bigl(1+\kappa_u\Delta u_t+\kappa_b b_t\bigr),
\quad
r_t=\frac{F^{\mathrm{twin}}_t+f}{\mathrm{free}_t+f},
\end{equation}
with a small positive shift $f$ so the ratio is defined when free stocks
cross zero, and $\eta$ symmetric in surplus and shortage (muting abundance
stored gluts: flex demand never cheapened enough to eat them). Then
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:pstar}
p^\star_t=\omega\,p^{\mathrm{tr}}_t+(1-\omega)\,p^{\mathrm{scar}}_t,
\qquad
p_t=\rho\,p_{t-1}+(1-\rho)\,p^\star_t,
\end{equation}
clipped to $[60,1200]$ \$/t. If free, unmet anomaly, and blockage match the
twin (\textbf{calm}), $p^\star_t=p_0$ by construction
(\texttt{assert\_twin\_identity}). That is why the unperturbed baseline is a
horizontal line at $p_0$, unlike Agrimate's seasonal unperturbed run.

$\omega$, $\eta$, $\kappa_u$, $\kappa_b$, $\lambda$, $\rho$, and the ask
gains are reduced-form and sign-constrained. They are shared across years
and are \textbf{not} fit crisis-by-crisis.

\subsection{Orchestration}
\label{sec:orch}

\texttt{run\_crop\_dynamics} (i)~clips seed stocks from PSD year
\texttt{stock\_seed\_year} (2005 for 2006--11; 2020 for 2021--23);
(ii)~spins two climatology years with $\tau=0$ and industrial off;
(iii)~runs the twin; (iv)~runs the treatment with the chosen
\texttt{use\_amis}/\texttt{use\_shocks}/\texttt{use\_demand}/\texttt{use\_industrial}
flags. Official matched P1 is harvest on, AMIS on, mean flex, industrial on
for USA maize.
