# A2d — the conditional and the attribution table

The `tau` column of the attribution table comes from the
restriction-only leg (use_amis=True, use_shocks=False). The
conditional fires in that leg. Below: where it fires relative to the
scoring windows, and what the ratios become without it.

## wheat

- conditional fires at 26/144 steps of the restriction-only leg
- first fires 2006-01, last 2007-01; contiguous from the start: yes
- 2007/08: tau hike as published **x1.70**, without the conditional **x1.99** (observed x1.82)
- 2010/11: tau hike as published **x1.36**, without the conditional **x1.36** (observed x1.16)
- 2007/08 base window level: pinned 213.5 vs unpinned 186.3 $/t (p0 = 213.5)

## maize

- conditional fires at 32/144 steps of the restriction-only leg
- first fires 2006-01, last 2007-04; contiguous from the start: yes
- 2007/08: tau hike as published **x1.10**, without the conditional **x1.69** (observed x1.84)
- 2010/11: tau hike as published **x0.92**, without the conditional **x0.86** (observed x1.44)
- 2007/08 base window level: pinned 135.4 vs unpinned 98.2 $/t (p0 = 135.4)

## rice

- conditional fires at 24/144 steps of the restriction-only leg
- first fires 2006-01, last 2006-12; contiguous from the start: yes
- 2007/08: tau hike as published **x1.75**, without the conditional **x1.70** (observed x1.84)
- 2010/11: tau hike as published **x0.81**, without the conditional **x0.81** (observed x0.79)
- 2007/08 base window level: pinned 339.0 vs unpinned 349.9 $/t (p0 = 339.0)

## Reading

If the published tau ratio is materially above the unpinned one, the
attribution number is partly an artifact of comparing a pinned base
against an unpinned peak, and the restriction-led claim for that crop
needs restating. A1c's 'no scored number depends on the conditional'
was too strong: it holds for the price correlation and for the full
and harvest-only hike ratios, not for the tau column.
