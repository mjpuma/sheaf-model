FAOSTAT bilateral trade matrices (E0) and the country conversion table, sourced
from the FoodTradeNetwork repository (mjpuma/FoodTradeNetwork, inputs_processed/
and ancillary/). E0[i,j] is the bilateral flow from exporter i to importer j for
the given crop and year window; rows are exporter names, columns FAOSTAT area
codes. Absolute magnitudes are treated as unit-agnostic (use for structure;
rescale to a USDA total for magnitude).

Vendored here as a runnable sample for Gate 0 per-crop spines:
  - Wheat / Maize / Rice baseline (2019-2021 Avg E0)
  - Crisis windows 2006-2007 and 2010-2011 Avg E0 for each of those three grains

Copied from FoodTradeNetwork `inputs_processed/` (2026-08-24). Other
crops/windows still load from a local FoodTradeNetwork checkout via `data_dir`.

NOTE: production (P0) and reserves (R0) are intentionally NOT taken from FAOSTAT
(its stocks are food-balance residuals); SHEAF sources those from USDA PSD.
