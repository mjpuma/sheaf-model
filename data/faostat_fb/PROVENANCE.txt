FAOSTAT Food Balance Sheets, historic domain FBSH
(Food Balances (-2013, old methodology and population)).

What is here
  wheat_fbsh_2006_2011.csv       wheat item 2511, years 2006–2011, wide
  wheat_fbsh_2006_2011_long.csv  same rows, one element per line
  DOWNLOAD_META.json             fetch timestamp and source URL

What is not here
  The bulk zip is gitignored (regenerate with --faostat-fb).
  Agrimate's unpublished local wheat_food_balance_fao.csv is still not
    shipped. A labelled host reconstruction of impute_food_balance_fao
    lives in data/food_balances/ (not bit-identical; not adopted).
    This extract is the official FAOSTAT dump, the FBS input to that
    reconstruction, not the author's local product.
  FoodTradeNetwork 2015–21 P0/R0 averages are not copied.
  FBS 2010+ (new methodology) is not mixed into this extract.

Source
  https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/FBSH
  Bulk: https://bulks-faostat.fao.org/production/FoodBalanceSheetsHistoric_E_All_Data_(Normalized).zip
  Item: 2511 Wheat and products. Unit: 1000 t.
  Elements kept: production, food, feed, seed, losses, imports, exports,
  domestic_supply, stock_variation.
  Stock Variation is FBSH element 5074 (FBS new methodology uses 5072).
  It is a food-balance residual, not USDA ending stocks.
