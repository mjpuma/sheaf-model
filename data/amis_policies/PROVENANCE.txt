AMIS / OECD export-restriction policy database
==============================================

What this is
------------
The **OECD Database on Export Restrictions on Staple Crops** — OECD’s contribution
to the G20 **Agricultural Market Information System (AMIS) Policy Database**.
It is the canonical public source for historical export bans/taxes/quotas on
wheat, maize, rice, and soybeans used in Agrimate / SHEAF Level-1–2 validation.

Official pages
--------------
OECD topic + download:
  https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/agro-food-trade/export-restrictions-on-staple-crops.html

AMIS Policy Database introduction / query-download:
  https://www.amis-outlook.org/policy-database/introduction
  https://www.amis-outlook.org/policy-database/query-download

Methodology: OECD (2024), https://doi.org/10.1787/afad5112-en

Fetched / installed
-------------------
Installed 2026-08-22. Unattended HTTPS fetch of oecd.org is blocked by Cloudflare
bot protection from this environment. The workbook vendored here is the public
OECD/AMIS export-restrictions database (sheets DetailedDatabase +
AggregatedDatabase; ~472 KB, consistent with the site’s “Download the database
(XLSX, ~516 KB)” listing).

To refresh from the website in a browser:
  1. Open the OECD topic page above.
  2. Use “Download the database (XLSX)”.
  3. Replace oecd_export_restrictions_staple_crops.xlsx in this folder.
  4. Re-run: python scripts/fetch_external_data.py --amis-only

Coverage (this snapshot)
------------------------
- Export restrictions from 2007 onward (start/end dates per measure)
- Commodities: Wheat, Maize, Rice, Soybeans (+ some mixed classes)
- AMIS participating countries that recorded measures in the window
- Aggregated sheet collapses duplicate HS-line detail for trend analysis;
  Detailed sheet retains HS codes, values, conditions, legal links

SHEAF use
---------
Level 1: exogenous τ forcing from observed restriction episodes.
Level 2: ground truth for who restricted (and roughly when/how severely).
