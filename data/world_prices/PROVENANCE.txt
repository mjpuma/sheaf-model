World Bank Pink Sheet — annual grain prices (Level-1 target)
============================================================

Source
------
World Bank Commodity Markets “Pink Sheet” historical annual workbook:

  https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets

Current workbook URL is discovered from that page (hash changes monthly):

  …/related/CMO-Historical-Data-Annual.xlsx

License: World Bank open data terms (see Commodity Markets page).

Fetched
-------
See DOWNLOAD_META.json for the UTC timestamp of this install.

Contents in this directory
--------------------------
CMO-Historical-Data-Annual.xlsx     Official annual Pink Sheet workbook
pink_sheet_grains_annual.csv        Wheat / rice / maize annual extract
pink_sheet_grains_monthly.csv       Monthly extract (nominal → real via annual
                                    real/nominal ratio within each year; Pink
                                    Sheet monthly workbook is nominal-only)
CMO-Historical-Data-Monthly.xlsx    Official monthly Pink Sheet workbook
DOWNLOAD_META.json                  Machine-readable fetch record
PROVENANCE.txt                      This file

Series mapping (Pink Sheet → SHEAF grain)
-----------------------------------------
wheat  ← “Wheat, US HRW”     ($/mt, U.S. hard red winter, Gulf)
rice   ← “Rice, Thai 5%”     ($/mt, Thailand 5% broken, f.o.b. Bangkok)
maize  ← “Maize”             ($/mt)

Units / deflation
-----------------
price_nominal_usd_mt     — nominal USD per metric tonne
price_real_2010_usd_mt   — constant 2010 USD, deflated by the World Bank
                           Manufacturers’ Unit Value (MUV) index
                           (sheet “Annual Prices (Real)” in the workbook)

Level-1 validation uses the **real** series as the observed world-price target.
**Gate 0** scores **monthly** wheat (`pink_sheet_grains_monthly.csv`,
`load_price_series_monthly`) against the 24-step spine.

Refresh
-------
  python scripts/fetch_external_data.py --prices-only
